# V4.1 method and limits

V4.1 retains the V3 constrained NSGA-II operators and V4 real GeoJSON parcel and module grammar. It adapts Riskiyanto et al.'s Figures 1 and 4 without depending on Rhino, Grasshopper, Kangaroo or Wallacei. The paper supplies a computational method, not a fixed architectural plan. All design thresholds below are `[DESIGN HYPOTHESIS]` unless stated otherwise.

## Site evidence

The source parcel is `data/site.geojson`. Its edge 7, from `(107.0082724, -6.3505665)` to `(107.008038, -6.350512)`, is the user-verified public arrival zone. The full segment is used for distance and access attraction; no midpoint is asserted as a gate. Geographic north comes from the supplied coordinates. The road hierarchy, service access, TPST-facing edge, settlement edge, wind, noise, odour, dust, flood/drainage, vegetation, obstacles, landscape opportunities and preferred expansion direction remain `[TO VERIFY]`. Those unknown fields return `null` and are excluded from placement and fitness. A service route may still be arranged internally, but it is not scored against a fabricated external entrance.

The continuous fields currently active are distance-based public access attraction, domestic protection depth from the arrival edge, and community interface suitability derived from public access. Solar orientation is a coordinate reference; the daylight score is a schematic facade-orientation proxy, not a measured daylight simulation. If an evidenced contextual geometry is added later, the corresponding field may be enabled explicitly.

## Unit population and program

The module catalogue records function, meaningful size/program variant, developmental or variational modularity, component counts, nominal room capacity, resident capacity and staff capacity. Repeatability is an independent property. R4 household variants differ in sleeping territories, WASH, domestic common rooms and retreat edges. Care, commons, learning, livelihood, community and transition variants similarly change composition. Component function is tagged from the PA5 corpus where supported; quantities and spatial arrangement are design hypotheses.

Each chromosome has an explicit count gene for every functional family. A separate population-seed gene determines packing jitter. The archive stores requested and retained counts by function and variant, developmental/variational unit ratios and a specialized-function ratio (B0 care/safeguarding plus I0 reflection units). This specialized definition is an architectural adaptation, not the paper's office-manager subgroup.

Nominal room capacity is the sum of simultaneous places across the selected room schedule. A demand scenario requests one **concurrent day-user population**; the model allocates those people among available functions in one simultaneous snapshot. This deliberately does not sum A0, B0, C0, H0, J0, K0, I0 and L0 capacities as if their users were all distinct. Resident population comes from R4 capacity. Staff numbers are a schematic concurrent staffing scenario bounded by nominal staff capacity; operational staffing must be developed separately.

## Architectural packing and zoning

One chromosome encodes module variant, raw position, rotation, group ID, attachment mode, shared edge, connection type, floor and zone offset. Packing searches nonoverlapping positions inside the exact polygon. Relationship grammar permits specified threshold, porch, service, circulation and court interfaces. A linear cluster seeks shared edges; courtyard and wing arrangements preserve a gap or angled relationship; detached layouts keep independent targets. The grouping pattern and per-unit genes alter actual placement. A stacked unit needs a compatible ground support footprint. These are schematic module footprints, not developed plans.

Five zone-depth genes divide the polygon into ordered public/civic, controlled care, shared everyday, domestic and personal bands. The service network runs in parallel; it is not the final step of the privacy sequence. The band areas are clipped against the parcel polygon. A **balanced zone** has no band covering 40% of parcel area, a **dominated zone** has a largest band covering 40–65%, and a **total zone** has a largest band covering at least 65%. These definitions adapt the paper's visual reading for the care campus.

After packing, a separate collision gate reports all positive-area same-floor overlaps. Shared edges have zero overlap area and count as intentional attachments only when the module grammar, group and relationship state allow them. Placement avoids unpermitted touching; an independent hard gate catches any that remains. Cross-floor footprint intersections are recorded separately and checked for support. Unit filtration reports generated, retained and filtered units with reasons. It can remove an unplaceable unit, but essential-program, residential and occupancy constraints still operate on retained units. `MODEL_FEASIBLE` requires zero unintended overlap and zero unpermitted shared-edge contact, contained retained footprints, essential A0/B0/C0/F0/M0/R4 functions, 8–32 residents, satisfaction of the concurrent day-user demand, compatible zoning, valid stacking and consistent filtration counts. Schematic setbacks are soft; this label does not establish code compliance.

## Fitness and selection

Six normalized objective families are maximized: care/safeguarding; privacy/domesticity; everyday/community; access/service; site/environment; adaptability/phasing. Each archive record also stores care and amenity distances, privacy and sightline proxies, route lengths and conflicts, entry relationship, landscape continuity, courtyard quality, orientation proxy, stacking share, usable vacant growth reserve and phase viability. The site score uses distance to the true arrival segment and domestic depth, rather than a reward for proximity to any parcel edge. Verified environmental or service-edge terms enter only if their layer is enabled.

All evaluated births, not only final survivors, are archived with exact genotype, seed, birth and last selected generation, module/zone/collision/filtration states, objective vector and constraints. All-generation and final-generation Pareto sets are distinct. Per-objective fittest, rank-relative-difference, average-fitness-rank and architectural balanced selections are distinct modes. Average-linkage hierarchical clustering simplifies the all-generation Pareto field; a quantitative K-means comparison is a secondary diagnostic and is not claimed as paper-equivalent.

The schematics still need plan and section design, screening studies, actual circulation widths and gradients, structural stacking, local planning/code review, flood/environmental evidence and operator review. Search cells with no feasible solution found are recorded as **not found under the stated search budget**; they do not prove that architecture cannot solve the brief.
